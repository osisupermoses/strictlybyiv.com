How payment works

Your money waits until the job is done.

You pay through StrictlyByIV, not into a stranger's account. We hold it, and the provider is paid after they've delivered.

  1. 1

    Book and pay

    Agree the price in the app, then pay a deposit or the full amount, whichever the provider asks for.

    Held by StrictlyByIV
  2. 2

    Pay any balance

    If you paid a deposit, settle the rest before the event. Every payment shows up on the booking.

    Still held
  3. 3

    Enjoy your event

    Your provider delivers. If something goes wrong, open a dispute from the booking before the payment is released.

    Disputes pause payment
  4. 4

    Provider gets paid

    Payment is released after a short protection window once your event ends, or sooner if you confirm everything went well.

    Paid only to verified providers

Questions

Good to know

Is StrictlyByIV free?

Yes. The app is free to download and planning is free for hosts, with room for up to 500 guests per event. You only pay for the bookings you make.

Do my guests need the app?

No. Each guest gets a personal invitation link that opens in any browser. They reply there, and if they're coming, they get a QR entry pass for the door.

Can more than one person check guests in?

Yes. Anyone on your team with access to the guest list can scan passes at the same time, at different gates. A pass that has already been used is flagged.

Where is it available?

Providers can list from anywhere in Nigeria. You can plan from anywhere too, including if you're organising an event back home from abroad.

What happens if a provider doesn't deliver?

Open a dispute from the booking before the payment is released. The payment is put on hold while our team reviews it.

How do I list my business?

Download the app, create an account and set up your provider profile. Once it's reviewed, clients can find and book you.

As a provider, do I have to wait until after the event to get paid?

No. Once the booking is confirmed, you can request a payout any time, even before the event, and your client can release part of the payment early or all of it by confirming the job is done. If they do nothing, it is released automatically after a short protection window once the event ends. The details are in our payment, refund and payout policy.
