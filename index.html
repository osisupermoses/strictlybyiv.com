How payment works

Your money waits until the job is done.

You pay through StrictlyByIV, not into a stranger's account. We hold it, and the provider is paid after they've delivered.

  1. 1

    Book and pay a deposit

    Agree the price in the app and pay the deposit securely through StrictlyByIV.

    Held by StrictlyByIV
  2. 2

    Pay the balance

    Settle the rest before the event. Every payment shows up on the booking.

    Still held
  3. 3

    Enjoy your event

    Your provider delivers. If something goes wrong, open a dispute from the booking before the payment is released.

    Disputes pause payment
  4. 4

    Provider gets paid

    Payment is released 24 hours after your event ends, or sooner if you confirm everything went well.

    Paid only to verified providers

Questions

Good to know

Is StrictlyByIV free?

Yes. The app is free to download and planning is free for hosts, with room for up to 500 guests per event. You only pay for the bookings you make.

Where is it available?

Providers can list from anywhere in Nigeria. You can plan from anywhere too, including if you're organising an event back home from abroad.

What happens if a provider doesn't deliver?

Open a dispute from the booking before the payment is released. The payment is put on hold while our team reviews it.

How do I list my business?

Download the app, create an account and set up your provider profile. Once it's reviewed, clients can find and book you.
