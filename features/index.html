Features

Everything in one app.

StrictlyByIV started with invitations and grew into the whole event: budget, checklist and team, booking planners, vendors and venues, RSVPs, live check-in at the gate and chat. Here is everything it does.

  • Planning
  • Guests & check-in
  • Booking
  • Team & chat
  • For providers
  • Your account

Planning

Budget, checklist and every event in one place.

Run several events at once, from weddings and birthdays to naming ceremonies, burials, graduations, concerts, conferences and corporate events, including events that run over several days.

Budgeting

Set a target, split it into categories, and track each item as planned, quoted, committed, paid or outstanding. Booking payments flow in automatically, and you are warned when you go over budget or a payment is overdue.

Checklists

Every event starts with tasks for its type. Set due dates, assign tasks to people on your team, and get a reminder on the day.

Your events

Cover photo, location and map for each event. Keep it private, or make it public with its own shareable page.

Guests & check-in

RSVPs and a smooth gate on the day.

Your guests do not need the app. Everything they see opens in their browser.

Guest list

Add guests with plus-ones, categories such as VIP, table numbers and notes. Import from a CSV file or your phone contacts, and export whenever you need to.

Invitations & RSVPs

Each guest gets a personal invitation link to reply attending, maybe or declined, with plus-ones, dietary notes and a message. Set an RSVP deadline and a dress code, and keep the address hidden until they accept.

Live guest check-in

Guests who are coming get a QR entry pass. Scan passes at the gate, or check guests in by name, with several people checking in at once. A pass that has already been used is flagged, and you can follow arrivals live.

Booking

Find, compare and book the people who make it happen.

Planners, vendors across 39 kinds of trade, and venues across 25 kinds of space, all over Nigeria.

Find providers

Search and filter by category, state and LGA, price, venue capacity, and verified identity or registered business. See packages, prices, photos, policies, FAQs and reviews.

Book in the app

Send a booking request, and agree the price with a counter-offer if needed. Pay in full or pay a deposit now and the balance later, through Paystack.

Payment held

Your payment is held until you release it, or until after the event. Release it early when you are happy, and open a dispute from the booking if something goes wrong.

Reviews

After a booking, rate the provider and leave a review that helps the next person choose.

Team & chat

Plan together, and keep every conversation in one place.

Bring in family, co-hosts, committees and your planner.

Team collaboration

Invite people by phone, email or a join link, and choose what each person can see and do: guests, checklist, budget, bookings and chat. Editors make changes, viewers follow along.

Chat

Message providers one-to-one or inside a booking, and talk with your team in the event group chat. Share photos, files and video, reply to messages and react.

Notifications

Push notifications for bookings, payments, tasks and messages, and reply to a message straight from the notification.

For providers

For planners, vendors and venues.

List your business, get booked and get paid.

Your listing

Packages with prices, units and add-ons, a photo gallery, FAQs and your policies. Venues show their type, capacity and amenities such as generator, borehole water and parking.

Bookings & payouts

Accept, decline or counter-offer booking requests, chat with clients, and get paid to your Nigerian bank account.

Trust & reach

Verify your identity or registered business for a badge clients trust, collect reviews, and share your own web page. Pro adds profile analytics, more listings and photos, and featured placement.

Your account

One account for everything.

Host your own events and run your business from the same account.

One account, every role

Plan events as a host and be a planner, a vendor and a venue at the same time.

Sign in your way

Email, phone number or Google, and Apple on iPhone.

Safety & control

Light and dark mode, block and report people, choose your privacy settings, and delete your account from the app.
