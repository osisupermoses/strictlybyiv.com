Community Guidelines

StrictlyByIV is built for trust: planners, providers, collaborators, guests, and clients should be able to coordinate without abuse, fraud, or unsafe behavior.

Document status In effect

Effective date: October 5, 2026

1. Treat people with respect

Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, shame, exploit, or discriminate against other users, providers, guests, collaborators, or StrictlyByIV team members.

2. Be accurate and honest

Profiles, listings, event details, booking requests, guest information, reviews, media, and messages should be truthful and not misleading.

Do not create fake reviews, fake profiles, false availability, fake payment claims, or misleading service descriptions.

3. Keep the platform safe

  • Do not upload malware, spam, or abusive content.
  • Do not attempt to bypass account, payment, booking, or access controls.
  • Do not use StrictlyByIV to coordinate illegal, harmful, or exploitative activity.

4. Marketplace standards

Providers should keep marketplace profiles, media, packages, pricing context, payout details, and availability information current. Bookers should only send serious booking requests with accurate event information.

5. Enforcement

StrictlyByIV may limit, remove, delist, suspend, or review accounts, profiles, events, bookings, messages, reviews, or payments when platform rules appear to be violated.

6. Contact

Reports or questions about community standards can be sent to support@strictlybyiv.com.
