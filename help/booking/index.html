Booking Help

Use this guide to understand booking requests, booking statuses, payments, cancellation review, completion, and booking messages.

1. How bookings start

Bookings on StrictlyByIV are tied to an event. When you request a planner, vendor, or venue from an event workspace, the app can start the booking request for that event directly.

If you find a provider from global Explore, the app may first ask you to choose an existing event or create one. This keeps the request connected to the event date, location, scope, and team context.

  • Submitting a request does not mean payment has been taken.
  • The provider reviews the request before paid steps become available.
  • Request details can include amount, service dates, description, and notes.
  • Provider-controlled terms, such as payment mode and deposit percentage, come from the provider profile.

2. Booking statuses

The app shows the current booking state and only displays actions that are available for that booking.

  • Pending: The request is waiting for provider review. You may be able to edit, cancel, or message depending on the actions shown.
  • Accepted: The provider has accepted the proposed terms and the booker can continue to any required payment step.
  • Confirmed: The booking has moved into an active operational state after the required payment or confirmation rules are satisfied.
  • Declined: The provider declined the request. The booking remains available for history.
  • Cancelled: The booking has ended before completion. Available refund or review details depend on payment state and policy rules.
  • Completed: The service has been marked complete according to the app's completion flow.
  • Under review: The booking needs support or dispute review before it can continue or close.

3. Payments and refunds

Payment steps appear only when the booking is ready for them. Depending on the provider's setup, a booking may require full upfront payment or a deposit with a later balance.

StrictlyByIV uses hosted payment checkout when payment is required. After checkout, the app checks the latest payment schedule and booking state before showing the next action.

  • Use the payment action shown in the booking detail screen when it is available.
  • Do not send payment outside the app for an in-app booking request.
  • Refund windows and held-funds rules are policy-controlled and may depend on timing, payment status, and review state.
  • Read Payments, Refunds, and Payouts

4. Cancellation and disputes

If self-serve cancellation is available, the booking detail screen shows a cancellation action. This usually means the booking can be cancelled without support review, such as before successful payment or within an eligible refund window.

If the booking is paid and no longer self-serve cancellable, the app may show an open-dispute or support-review action instead. Use that path when the booking needs help beyond normal cancellation.

  • Review the confirmation copy before cancelling or opening a dispute.
  • When a booking is under review, paid funds may remain held while support reviews the issue.
  • Cancelled bookings may still remain visible for history, payment records, or support context.

5. Completion

Completion actions help both sides confirm that service delivery is finished. A booker may confirm service delivery, and a provider may mark service delivered when they are ready.

When both sides confirm, eligible held funds may release sooner. If service is incomplete, incorrect, or needs review, do not confirm completion; message the other party or open support review instead.

6. Messages

Booking-linked messages are for context and coordination. Booking actions such as accept, decline, cancel, pay, complete, or dispute stay inside the booking screens.

If you contact support, include the booking ID if it is visible in the app.
